# 04 — Risk Register

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Rating | Mitigation | Contingency | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stakeholder resistance | Medium | High | High | Early engagement and change communication | Escalate decision blockers to sponsor | PM |
| Baseline data gaps | High | High | High | Validate data sources and document limitations | Use approved sampling/assumptions | PM + Data |
| Scope creep | Medium | High | High | Formal scope boundaries and change control | Defer non-critical requests | PM |
| Implementation disruption | Medium | High | High | Phased rollout and UAT | Roll back affected change | Process Owner |
| KPI adoption delays | Medium | Medium | Medium | Define owners and reporting cadence early | Manual interim reporting | Data Owner |

## Risk Management Approach

Risks are reviewed during project status updates and milestone reviews. High-rated risks receive an identified owner, mitigation action, contingency response, and monitoring trigger.

## Escalation Trigger

Escalate when a risk threatens a milestone, materially changes scope, creates compliance/quality exposure, or requires a decision outside the Project Manager's authority.
