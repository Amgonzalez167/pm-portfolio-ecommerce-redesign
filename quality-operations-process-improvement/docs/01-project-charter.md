# 01 — Project Charter

## Project Overview

**Project:** Quality Operations Process Improvement  
**Project Manager:** Amneris Gonzalez Peña  
**Project Type:** Hypothetical portfolio simulation

## Business Problem

A quality-sensitive operational workflow is experiencing recurring defects, rework, inconsistent execution, cycle-time variation, and control gaps. Leadership needs a structured improvement initiative with measurable outcomes and clear ownership.

## Objective

Design and implement a standardized future-state workflow that improves quality, reduces rework, strengthens process controls, and creates a repeatable KPI reporting model.

## In Scope

- Current-state process mapping
- Stakeholder analysis and RACI
- Baseline quality and cycle-time metrics
- Root-cause analysis
- Future-state workflow and controls
- UAT planning and defect management
- KPI definitions and targets
- Project closure and lessons learned

## Out of Scope

- Enterprise-wide technology replacement
- Changes to unrelated business processes
- Production performance claims outside the simulation
- Long-term operational ownership after transition

## Success Criteria

- Current-state process validated by process stakeholders
- Baseline metrics documented with assumptions and limitations
- Major root causes identified and validated
- Future-state workflow approved
- UAT scenarios and sign-off criteria established
- KPI targets and reporting cadence documented
- Closure criteria and operational handoff completed

## Governance

The Project Manager coordinates scope, schedule, risks, dependencies, stakeholder communication, documentation, and status reporting. Process owners validate operational requirements and business acceptance.

## Key Assumptions

- Relevant stakeholders are available for review sessions.
- Baseline data can be accessed and validated.
- Process owners can approve future-state requirements.
- UAT participants are available before rollout.

## Key Constraints

- Limited historical data quality
- Stakeholder availability
- Potential resistance to process change
- Need to minimize operational disruption

## Milestones

1. Project Initiation Complete
2. Current-State & Baseline Complete
3. Future-State Design Approved
4. UAT & Implementation Complete
5. KPI Validation & Project Closure
