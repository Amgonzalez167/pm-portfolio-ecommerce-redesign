# 01 — Project Charter & Scope

## Project
**E-Commerce Mobile App Redesign**

**Project Manager:** Amneris Gonzalez Peña  
**Methodology:** Agile / Scrum  
**Target outcome:** Reduce mobile cart abandonment from 38% to 22% while improving checkout usability.

## Business Case
The redesign addresses friction in product discovery, cart review, and checkout. The project is structured as a portfolio case study demonstrating project planning, stakeholder management, risk management, sprint delivery, status reporting, and retrospective practice.

## Objectives
- Reduce cart abandonment from 38% to 22%.
- Improve checkout completion and mobile usability.
- Establish measurable acceptance criteria for the redesigned experience.
- Deliver a controlled six-sprint implementation plan.

## In Scope
- Mobile product-detail experience
- Cart and checkout flow
- UX/UI requirements and acceptance criteria
- Analytics/KPI definition
- QA/UAT coordination
- Release readiness and post-launch measurement

## Out of Scope
- Desktop redesign
- Full backend re-platforming
- New payment processor implementation
- International expansion

## Key Deliverables
1. Approved project charter and scope baseline
2. Product backlog and six-sprint roadmap
3. RAID log and mitigation plans
4. Weekly executive status dashboard
5. QA/UAT readiness and launch checklist
6. Retrospective and KPI review

## Success Metrics
| KPI | Baseline | Target |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cart abandonment | 38% | 22% |
| Checkout completion | Baseline established in discovery | +20% relative improvement |
| Critical checkout defects | Baseline established during QA | 0 open at launch |

## Stakeholders
- Executive sponsor
- Product owner
- Project manager
- UX/UI designer
- Engineering team
- QA team
- Analytics/marketing stakeholders

## Governance
Weekly status reporting, sprint ceremonies, RAID review, scope-change control, and launch-readiness checkpoints are used to keep delivery transparent and aligned to business outcomes.
